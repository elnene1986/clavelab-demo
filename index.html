Our Story

A lifetime of listening to old things.

Jwan grew up among looms and copper, in a home where an object was never just an object — it was a memory you could hold.

For over two decades she has travelled the villages and bazaars of Kurdistan, sitting with weavers, silversmiths, and families letting go of heirlooms. She learned to read the language of wear: the softened edge of a much-loved bowl, the faded indigo of a wedding kilim, the initials scratched into a silver amulet.

Every piece in the shop is chosen by hand and comes with its story intact — where it was made, what it was for, and the people who kept it before you. Nothing here is mass-produced. Everything here has already lived a life.

— Jwan

Why It Matters

More than antiques —
a craft of care.

Authenticated by hand

Jwan personally sources and vets every item. You get provenance, an honest age estimate, and the story behind the piece — never a guess dressed up as fact.

Heritage preserved

Kurdish weaving and silverwork are living traditions. Every sale helps keep these crafts — and the families who still practice them — alive and valued.

Cared for, not cleaned up

We conserve rather than "restore." The patina, the mends, the honest wear — that's the history. We keep it, and we tell you exactly what's original.
